## Case 1 --- Payment support operations

**My classification: Minimal risk**

A payment company receives thousands of support requests from merchants
every week and wants to reduce the time agents spend sorting them
manually. An AI system analyses incoming support tickets and classifies
them into categories, such as integration problems, payout questions,
account configuration or technical incidents. It routes each ticket to
the appropriate internal team and suggests a priority based on
operational information, such as whether a payment integration is
currently unavailable. Support agents can change the category, priority,
or routing at any time. The system does not decide whether merchants can
access financial products or services.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Case 2 --- Employee coaching platform

**My classification: High-risk**

A corporate coaching platform wants to help managers better support
employee development between formal performance reviews. Employees
regularly interact with an AI coach about their goals, workplace
challenges, progress and relationships at work. The system analyses
these conversations together with goal completion and coaching activity
to identify individual strengths, development areas and signs that
progress may be falling behind. Managers receive a monthly dashboard
with individual insights that they can use during one-to-ones and
performance conversations. Managers remain responsible for decisions
such as promotions, performance plans or changes in responsibilities.
Employees are informed that they are interacting with an AI coach.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Case 3 --- Beauty & wellness app

**My classification: Prohibited**

A beauty and wellness app wants to increase conversion to its premium
skincare and coaching programmes. Users upload selfies, track appearance
goals and discuss their concerns with an AI assistant, which
personalises recommendations, notifications and coaching messages. The
company has found that more urgent and frequent messaging is
particularly effective among younger users and users who repeatedly
abandon purchases because of insufficient funds, so the AI uses these
signals when determining how and when to approach them. It may highlight
appearance concerns the user has previously expressed and suggest that
paid programmes could address them. Personalisation happens
automatically, while the growth team monitors overall conversion and
retention.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Case 4 --- Reality-TV companion

**My classification: Limited risk / transparency**

A streaming entertainment startup wants to launch an interactive
companion for reality-TV viewers. Users can chat with an AI assistant
about contestants, relationships and events from episodes they have
already watched. The system retrieves information from episode summaries
and other approved sources and generates conversational answers, recaps
and theories tailored to how far the viewer has progressed in the
season. It does not make decisions about users or determine their access
to content or services. Some responses are generated entirely by the AI
rather than retrieved directly from source material.
