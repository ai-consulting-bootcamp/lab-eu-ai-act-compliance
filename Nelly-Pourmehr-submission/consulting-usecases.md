Case 1
A retail bank wants a tool to score loan applicants using social media activity and browsing history, not just financial data. The tool would flag "high risk" applicants and auto-reject them without human review. The bank wants faster approvals and fewer bad loans.

Case 2
A hospital network wants a tool to help triage patients in the ER. The tool reads vitals and symptoms and suggests a priority level. A nurse can override the suggestion before it's acted on.

Case 3
An e-commerce company wants a chatbot on their site to answer order and return questions. The bot sounds natural and some customers think they're talking to a real person.

Case 4
A small design agency wants a tool that suggests color palettes and font pairings for client mockups. Designers review and approve every suggestion before use.
