# Private Answer Key

## Case 1

**Client brief for partner**
A manufacturing company wants to reduce unexpected production downtime by using AI to analyze machine sensor data and recommend preventive maintenance. Engineers review the recommendations before deciding whether maintenance should be performed.
___
Predictive Maintenance for Manufacturing Equipment
A manufacturing company that produces automotive components is looking for ways to reduce unexpected equipment failures because production stops are becoming increasingly expensive. They are considering an AI system that continuously analyzes data from sensors already installed on the production line, including vibration, temperature, energy consumption, and operating hours, and alerts maintenance staff when a machine appears likely to require servicing. Engineers would receive the recommendations through their maintenance dashboard and decide whether to inspect the equipment immediately or wait until the next scheduled maintenance window. The client believes this could reduce downtime without changing existing maintenance procedures.

**Intended category (private answer key)**
Minimal risk

**Why I chose it**
The AI is used for internal operational optimization and predictive maintenance. It does not make decisions affecting individuals' rights or opportunities and therefore does not trigger specific AI Act obligations beyond general legal requirements.

## Case 2

**Client brief for partner**
A regional hospital plans to introduce an AI-supported triage assistant that analyzes patient admission data and recommends treatment priority. Medical staff normally review the recommendation before assigning the final triage level.
___
AI-Supported Emergency Department Triage
A regional hospital is reviewing its emergency department workflow because waiting times have increased significantly during busy periods. One proposal is to introduce an AI-supported triage assistant that combines information already collected during patient admission, such as reported symptoms, vital signs, previous medical history, laboratory results when available, and ambulance notes, to recommend how urgently each patient should be seen. The recommendation would appear alongside the patient's record, and clinical staff would normally review it before assigning the final triage priority, although the hospital hopes the recommendations will become increasingly reliable over time. The outcome mainly affects patients waiting for emergency treatment.

**Intended category (private answer key)**
High-risk

**Why I chose it**
The AI supports decisions in healthcare that directly affect patients and access to medical services. It operates in a high-impact area where human oversight, documentation, and risk management are required under the AI Act.

## Case 3

**Client brief for partner**
An insurance company wants an AI assistant to answer customer questions about policies, claims, and required documentation while transferring complex cases to human support staff. The system provides information but does not make final insurance decisions.
___
AI Customer Support Assistant for Insurance Services
An insurance company wants to reduce the number of routine calls handled by its customer support team. It is exploring an AI assistant that customers can access through the company website to ask questions about insurance products, claims, required documents, and policy conditions. The assistant would generate responses using company documentation and previous conversations and would transfer the conversation to a human agent whenever customers request additional help or when the issue becomes more complex. The company says the assistant would provide information and guidance but would not approve claims or make final decisions on customer requests.

**Intended category (private answer key)**
Limited risk / Transparency

**Why I chose it**
Users interact directly with an AI system. The primary AI Act obligation is transparency, ensuring customers are informed they are communicating with AI.

## Case 4

**Client brief for partner**
A school district is considering an AI platform that analyzes classroom interaction patterns from online lessons to help teachers identify students who may benefit from additional support and improve lesson effectiveness. Teachers remain responsible for educational decisions.
___
AI-Assisted Classroom Participation Analytics
A school district is looking for ways to better understand why participation levels vary between online classes and to help teachers improve lesson delivery. A vendor has proposed an AI platform that analyzes webcam video, microphone audio, and interaction patterns during virtual lessons to generate classroom interaction indicators, participation trends, and lesson effectiveness metrics. The reports identify students who may benefit from additional attention and highlight sessions where participation appears lower than expected, allowing teachers to adapt their teaching approach. School administrators view the system as a teaching support tool rather than a student assessment system, while teachers remain responsible for all educational decisions and any follow-up with students.

**Intended category (private answer key)**
Prohibited

**Why I chose it**
Although presented as a teaching support tool, the system infers students' emotional or mental states in an educational setting from biometric and behavioral signals, which falls under the AI Act's prohibited practices.
