# Private answer key

## Case 1 — Minimal risk

**Why:** The AI helps prepare a commercial proposal by comparing documents and drafting text. A human expert checks the technical and commercial claims, confirms the supplier and price, and decides whether to send it. The AI supports the work but does not make a decision about a person or control safety-critical equipment.

**Main caution:** Check accuracy, confidentiality, and data protection. The AI Act category does not remove other legal or business obligations.

## Case 2 — Prohibited

**Why:** The system uses patients’ medical needs and financial hardship to target people with loans, then creates artificial urgency by claiming they could lose access to medicine even though the medicine remains available. This appears designed to exploit people’s vulnerability and pressure them into decisions that could cause serious financial or health-related harm. A human reviewing the messages does not fix the underlying policy.

**Decision:** Do not approve the proposed system. Redesign it so it provides neutral, accurate financing information without targeting vulnerability or using false urgency.

## Case 3 — High risk

**Why:** The system scores and ranks job applicants and recommends who should be interviewed. AI used to analyse or filter job applications or evaluate candidates is listed as high risk in the AI Act’s employment category. Human review is useful, but it does not by itself change the system’s intended use or remove the high-risk classification.

**Decision:** Approve only with appropriate safeguards, human oversight, documentation, and checks for bias and errors.

## Case 4 — Minimal risk

**Why:** The AI sorts internal project documents into predefined archive categories. It does not evaluate employees, make decisions about people, or change the documents. Uncertain classifications go to an administrator, who can correct them.

**Main caution:** Protect confidential documents, preserve access permissions, and make sure the administrator can correct filing mistakes.
