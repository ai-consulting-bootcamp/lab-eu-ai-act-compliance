## Overview

This repository contains my work for the **EU AI Act compliance consulting lab**. The lab focuses on turning client AI use cases into:

- an AI Act risk classification,
- a proposed AI architecture and operating model,
- a provider / deployer / vendor role map,
- required compliance controls, and
- a final consulting decision: **Approve**, **Approve with controls**, or **Deny and redesign**.

The work covers the four practical AI Act outcomes used in the lab:

1. **Prohibited**
2. **High-risk**
3. **Limited risk / transparency**
4. **Minimal risk**
