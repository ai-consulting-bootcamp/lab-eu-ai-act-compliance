# Lab 1 · Client briefs for blind review

## Case 1 · LindenDesk

- We run a Berlin customer-support centre and have a limited number of coaching slots.
- Team leads want to know who might benefit from a session after our weekly video role-plays.
- A vendor proposes analysing employees’ facial movements and vocal prosody in the recordings.
- Its dashboard would flag individual employees as frustrated, disengaged or anxious.
- Team leads would review and could override flags before offering coaching to the named staff.
- This is for staff development only. We are not addressing a medical or safety need.

## Case 2 · HafenWerk

- We service building equipment and receive around 300 applications when we open ten technician roles.
- An applicant-tracking vendor offers to rank applicants based on their CVs, certifications and screening answers.
- Recruiters start with the top 30, although they can scroll down and change the shortlist.
- Applicants below the initial cutoff may never be reviewed during a busy hiring round.
- HR signs the final interview list and would keep a record of any ranking changes.

## Case 3 · KiezRad

- We operate a bike-rental chain and want fewer routine tickets about returns and opening hours.
- We would add a conversational feature to our website using a hosted language model and approved help articles.
- The feature would answer customers in ordinary language and refer billing disputes to support.
- Support staff maintain the help articles and handle referrals, but they do not pre-approve each reply.
- Customers can use the feature without signing in. It cannot look up accounts, charge cards or change bookings.

## Case 4 · Tonwerk Supply

- We distribute ceramic supplies and have too many duplicate entries for glazes and kiln accessories.
- Warehouse operations wants a nightly process that groups similar item records for maintenance.
- A batch model would compare item names, dimensions, material and unit size.
- It would output candidate pairs to an internal queue. It would not generate descriptions or merge records.
- A catalog editor inspects each pair and decides what to correct. The system never evaluates staff or customers.
