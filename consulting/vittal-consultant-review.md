| 1 | **Prohibited** | Emotion/tone inference on employees in the workplace, feeding performance and promotion decisions, is exactly the practice Article 5(1)(f) bans. | 2 | **High-risk** | Automated CV screening/ranking that gates who a recruiter even sees, trained on historical hiring outcomes (which can encode past bias), falls under Annex III(4) employment/recruitment. Business affects access to employment for real people. | Trigger: CV + assessment submission. Behavior: model scores and tiers candidates. Human review: currently none required before auto-rejection of "not a fit" — this is the compliance gap. Output: tier label + auto-generated rejection or recruiter queue. Logging: needs an audit trail of scores and overrides. | Retailer = deployer (and likely provider if built in-house; vendor = provider if bought off-the-shelf — needs to be confirmed). | Risk management system, bias/data-quality testing on training data, human review of all auto-rejections (not just "strong fit"), transparency to candidates that AI is used, technical documentation, logging, registration obligations for the deployer once operational. | **Approve with controls** (mandatory human review before any auto-rejection, plus bias testing before launch). |
| 3 | **Limited risk / transparency** | A conversational system that can be mistaken for a human, holding real exchanges with consumers, triggers Article 50 disclosure duties. Behavior: LLM + retrieval answers, escalates on frustration/out-of-scope. Human review: live agent handles escalations and all binding actions (refunds).
