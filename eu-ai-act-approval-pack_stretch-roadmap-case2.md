# Stretch – Implementation roadmap: CV scoring for recruitment (Case 2, high-risk, Annex III 4(a))

*Annex III high-risk obligations apply from 2 Dec 2027 (Digital Omnibus). GDPR Art. 22, the AGG and German works council rights already apply.*

**Provider (US vendor), before placing on the EU market:**
- Appoint an EU authorised representative (Art. 22).
- Set up a risk management system (Art. 9) and a quality management system (Art. 17).
- Data governance (Art. 10): document the training data, and test for bias by gender, age and origin.
- Technical documentation (Art. 11), automatic logging (Art. 12) and instructions for use (Art. 13).
- Conformity assessment by internal control (Art. 43, Annex VI), EU declaration of conformity and CE marking (Arts. 47–48), and registration in the EU database (Art. 49).
- A post-market monitoring plan (Art. 72).

**Deployer (logistics company), before first use:**
- Inform the works council and staff (Art. 26(7)), and agree a works agreement covering BetrVG §§ 87 and 95.
- Carry out a DPIA (GDPR Art. 35).
- Appoint and train recruiters as human overseers (Arts. 4 and 26(2)). Write an oversight SOP: no rejection goes out without a recruiter's action.
- Remove the "top performer" profiles from the inputs. Match only against the job's stated requirements.
- Write an applicant notice (Art. 26(11)) and an explanation route (Art. 86).
- Configure log retention of at least 6 months (Art. 26(6)), and run a quarterly bias review.

**Evidence to request from the vendor:**
- EU declaration of conformity, EU database registration number and authorised representative details.
- Instructions for use, including accuracy metrics and known limitations.
- A summary of bias testing and the description of the training data.
- Logging specification, plus a DPA and the GDPR transfer mechanism (DPF or SCCs).
- An incident and model-change notification commitment in the contract.
