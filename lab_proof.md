## Phase 3 — Approval pack

**Launch assessment · 16 November 2026**

### Executive summary

WorkPulse should not launch as proposed. QuickLend and ShopHelp may proceed only after their launch conditions are met. FreshBake may launch with routine operational safeguards. These decisions concern the functions described; changes to purpose or decision authority require reassessment.

Under the current timetable, Annex III high-risk requirements apply from 2 December 2027. QuickLend’s controls below are launch recommendations and preparation for those duties, rather than a claim that every high-risk duty already applies. GDPR remains a separate launch condition. [Application timetable](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-113)

### Case 1 — WorkPulse

**Category: Prohibited · Decision: Deny and redesign**

WorkPulse infers employee emotions from webcam video and call audio, then supplies engagement scores for performance reviews and shift planning. Article 5(1)(f) prohibits workplace emotion inference except for medical or safety purposes. The described use does not establish either exception. Manager approval or worker notification cannot legalise it. [Article 5](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-5)

**Proposed redesign:** Call transcript → checks against observable service criteria → evidence-linked coaching feedback → supervisor review. Remove webcam analysis, emotional labels and emotion-derived scores. Record the evidence used and the supervisor’s corrections.

**Roles and conditions:** WorkPulse is the provider; client call centres are deployers. Confirm any hosting or model suppliers. If the replacement continues to evaluate employee performance, assess it under Annex III 4(b) before approval. Address lawful recording, worker information, access limits and retention. The replacement requires a fresh assessment; removing emotion inference does not complete compliance. [Annex III](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/annex-3)

### Case 2 — QuickLend

**Category: High-risk · Decision: Approve with controls**

Evaluating individuals’ creditworthiness falls under Annex III 5(b). Machine learning and small loan amounts do not remove this classification. [Annex III](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/annex-3)

**Architecture:** Application and validated financial inputs → versioned credit model → recorded score and reasons → lending decision. Refer uncertain or flagged cases to an authorised officer. Provide meaningful human reconsideration of contested decisions. Record outcomes and overrides, monitor errors and discrimination, and enable suspension.

**Roles:** QuickLend is the deployer. The provider is the entity supplying or putting the system into service under its own name—potentially QuickLend. Confirm the arrangement; external model and hosting suppliers are not automatically the system provider.

**Conditions:** Provider preparation must cover risk and data governance, testing, technical documentation, logging, oversight, accuracy, security and quality management. Complete the applicable conformity assessment, declaration, CE marking and registration when required; maintain post-market monitoring and incident procedures. QuickLend must prepare competent oversight, monitoring, appropriate log retention, applicant information, a FRIA before first use and notification of its results when applicable. [Provider duties](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-16), [deployer duties](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-26), [FRIA](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-27)

Before launch, validate performance and discrimination controls, establish complaints and review procedures, and assess GDPR Article 22 and DPIA requirements. Fully automated lending decisions require a valid legal route and applicable safeguards. Random inspection and accuracy tests alone are insufficient. [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679)

### Case 3 — ShopHelp

**Category: Transparency obligations (“limited risk”) · Decision: Approve with controls**

**Architecture:** Customer question → clear AI notice → retrieval from approved FAQs, product information and authenticated order data → answer or human handoff. Preserve the restriction against changing orders or approving refunds. Keep proportionate records of errors and escalations.

**Roles:** ShopHelp is the deployer. Confirm which entity supplies the chatbot under its name and acts as provider; identify model and hosting vendors separately.

**Conditions:** Article 50(1) requires customers to know they are interacting with AI unless this is already obvious. The provider must also assess generated-content marking under Article 50(2). Before launch, verify disclosure, restrict order access to the authenticated customer, test answer quality and handoff, and define retention and access controls. Blanket recording of every conversation is not an Article 50 requirement. [Article 50](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-50)

### Case 4 — FreshBake

**Category: Minimal risk · Decision: Approve**

**Architecture:** Daily sales, weather, holidays and local events → product-and-shop demand forecast → manager dashboard → adjusted or confirmed production order. Record forecast errors and overrides; provide a fallback when data is unavailable.

**Roles:** FreshBake is the deployer. The provider is the forecasting supplier operating under its own name, or FreshBake for an own-brand system. Confirm external data and hosting vendors; an LLM is unnecessary.

**Compliance implications:** This aggregate planning function does not establish a prohibited use, an Annex III application or a specific Article 50 trigger. Data-quality checks, editable orders and forecast monitoring are recommended operational practices, rather than high-risk compliance duties. Applicable general obligations and other laws remain relevant. Reassess before adding employee evaluation or safety-control functions. [Risk framework](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/faq?faq_category_id=90&page=0)

## Stretch practice — QuickLend Credit Scoring Implementation Roadmap

**Decision: Approve with controls.** Confirm the provider/deployer roles and launch date first; QuickLend may hold both roles. This roadmap targets full high-risk compliance, with Annex III duties applying from 2 December 2027 under the current timetable.

### 1. Provider — before market placement

Define intended use and limits. Complete risk management, data governance, technical documentation and quality management. Validate accuracy, discriminatory outcomes, robustness and cybersecurity; implement logs and effective human oversight. Complete the applicable conformity assessment, EU declaration, CE marking and registration. Establish post-market monitoring and incident procedures. For Annex III 5(b), conformity assessment follows internal control under Annex VI; mandatory third-party certification is not required.

### 2. QuickLend — before first use

Validate performance on the intended applicant population against agreed acceptance thresholds. Complete the FRIA and notify the market surveillance authority of its results when required; assess DPIA requirements separately. Train reviewers with authority to reconsider decisions and suspend use. Establish applicant notices, explanations, complaints, retention and monitoring procedures. Confirm the legal route and safeguards for fully automated lending decisions under GDPR Article 22.

### 3. Evidence to request from the vendor

Request version-specific conformity records and deployment instructions; data provenance and validation coverage; subgroup error and discrimination tests; security results; sample decision logs and explanations; and demonstrations of override and suspension functions. Obtain update, incident-notification and support commitments. A generic “AI Act compliant” statement is insufficient.

**Launch gate:** An accountable QuickLend owner signs off the evidence and confirms that unresolved risks are within agreed acceptance criteria before production use.
