## Part 2 - Use Cases Shared by Partner

### Executive summary

The travel assistant is a limited-risk use that can launch with a clear AI disclosure. The bakery forecast is a minimal-risk operational tool and can be approved with ordinary data, security and model-monitoring controls. The webcam and call-audio emotion analysis is prohibited when used for workplace performance management; human override does not cure the prohibition. The employee scheduling tool is high-risk because it supports task allocation and performance-related decisions about workers, so it requires provider evidence and controlled deployment before use.

### Decision notes

- **Travel assistant:** disclosure is a deployment boundary, not a substitute for accuracy. The assistant must not silently move from catalogue Q&A into booking, eligibility or personalised pricing.
- **Bakery forecast:** minimal risk under the AI Act does not remove GDPR, cybersecurity, food-safety or misleading-claim obligations if the workflow changes.
- **Emotion analysis:** a supervisor reviewing a prohibited score is still a human-in-the-loop around a prohibited system; it is not a compliance control.
- **Scheduling:** final human sign-off is necessary but insufficient. Workers need understandable information, meaningful oversight and a route to challenge material effects.
