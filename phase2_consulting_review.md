## Notes behind the calls

- **A vs. high-risk:** Annex III 5(a) covers *public authorities* assessing *natural persons'* eligibility for public benefits. A private consultancy ranking grants for companies is outside it.
- **B, the "medical reasons" argument:** the client may argue that high sick leave makes this a health use. The Commission's guidelines on prohibited practices reject general wellbeing and stress monitoring as a medical reason, so I don't accept the argument.
- **B, the redesign boundary:** inferring emotion from the *words* in a transcript isn't "emotion recognition" under Art. 3(39), because that definition requires biometric data. This is the Commission's reading and needs legal confirmation before the redesign is built.
- **D, the timing:** the client could legally launch before Dec 2027 without Annex III compliance. My advice is not to. They are retraining on past admission decisions, so any historical bias gets automated now, and GDPR and the AGG already apply.
