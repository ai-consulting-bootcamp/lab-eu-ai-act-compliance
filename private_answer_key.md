# EU AI Act — Private Answer Key

> Private working note. Do not share this file with the partner before the review.

## Case 1 — Prohibited

**Intended category:** Prohibited

**Why:** The system creates an ongoing behavioural profile of customers using signals gathered across different contexts and uses that profile to influence access to commercial benefits, financing and support. The case is designed to test the Article 5 social-scoring boundary rather than ordinary fraud or credit-risk assessment.

## Case 2 — High-risk

**Intended category:** High-risk

**Why:** The system is used to analyse, rank and filter candidates during recruitment. Because it materially affects access to employment and determines which applicants reach human review, it falls within the employment and recruitment use cases covered by Annex III.

## Case 3 — Limited risk / transparency

**Intended category:** Limited risk / transparency

**Why:** The AI system interacts directly with customers through a voice interface and therefore raises transparency obligations. Its current scope is limited to triage, information, summarisation and routing, while consequential commercial decisions are escalated to a human.

## Case 4 — Minimal risk

**Intended category:** Minimal risk

**Why:** The system summarises product-review information to support customer browsing but does not make decisions about individuals, access to services, employment, eligibility or other high-risk areas. No specific high-risk AI Act regime is triggered by the facts as designed.
