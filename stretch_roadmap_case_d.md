# Stretch – Mini Implementation Roadmap: Case D (Vocational Training Admissions)

Chosen as the strongest high-risk case: an Annex III 3(a) system, where the client moves into the provider role.

**Provider (the client, after rebranding and retraining) – before market placement / putting into service**
- A documented risk-management system and quality-management system.
- A data-governance file: provenance of the past admission data, a bias analysis across gender, age and origin proxies, and the corrections made.
- Technical documentation, accuracy metrics per applicant group, and automatic logging built in.
- Instructions for use, including the human-oversight design (the borderline band, sampling, time per file).
- Internal-control conformity assessment, EU declaration of conformity, CE marking, and registration in the EU database.
- A post-market monitoring plan and a serious-incident reporting route.

**Deployer (the client's admissions team) – before first use**
- Named, trained assessors with authority to override.
- The FRIA, if the "public service" reading is confirmed, plus a DPIA under GDPR.
- An applicant notice and an appeal process.
- Logs retained for at least six months.
- A one-cycle shadow run: the model ranks, humans decide blind, and the two are compared before the model influences any real decisions.

**Evidence to request from the EdTech vendor**
- Model card and training-data description.
- Validation and bias test results.
- Existing technical documentation and any conformity work.
- Logging capability.
- Contractual cooperation under Art. 25(2), and confirmation of which changes count as a "substantial modification".
