## Self-Correction Notes (Own Reasoning Process)

Classifications on Anand's four cases were directionally correct on first pass in all four instances. Two corrections were needed to the *reasoning*, not the label:

- **Case 1:** initially cited "Annex III" when the correct basis was Article 5 (prohibited practices are governed by Article 5, not Annex III, which is the high-risk list). The mechanism was also refined from "state inference" to the more accurate "social scoring."
- **Case 2:** initially framed the high-risk trigger as depending on whether human oversight would be overridden. Corrected to recognize that Annex III recruitment/screening is the trigger on its own, independent of oversight quality — oversight quality is a separate question about whether a required control is genuinely functioning.

This reinforced a general lesson applied consistently across the portfolio: **risk classification and human-oversight quality are two separate judgments.** Weak oversight doesn't change a case's classification (Case 2 in my own set); strong oversight doesn't create a low-risk classification on its own either (Case 4 in my own set). Both need to be assessed independently.
